# TerraFly claims ledger

This ledger separates what was demonstrated on the packaged build from what remains conditional. A judge claim is permitted only at the level shown here.

| Status | Claim | Evidence | Limitation | Judge wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PROVED | The packaged backend passes its automated suite. | 40 tests passed on 2026-08-28; `backend/tests/` | Tests use synthetic fixtures for scientific values. | "The packaged backend passes 40 contract, security, terrain, calibration and artifact tests." |
| PROVED | The packaged frontend builds and passes component/geometry tests. | 2 Vitest files, 10 tests; production build, `frontend/src/*.test.*` | Browser automation cannot prove every GPU/browser combination. | "The interface and measurement geometry pass 10 automated tests and a production build." |
| PROVED | DEM Terrain preserves supplied metric elevations through alignment and export. | `terrain.py:84-192,213-348`; `test_terrain.py:65-137`; live DEM smoke | Accuracy is inherited from the source DEM; resampling adds no information. | "TerraFly aligns and visualizes a named DEM without claiming that resampling improves its native accuracy." |
| PROVED | Photo AI uses the real Depth Anything V2 Large checkpoint on CUDA when available. | `depth_anything_v2.py:11-158`; verified revision `7581137eff8d4e94f6e796d3baea0e9fa79b22d2`, device CUDA | It is a general relative-depth model, not an overhead height model. | "Our current AI baseline is the real DAV2 Large checkpoint, recorded by exact revision; its output remains relative until calibrated." |
| PROVED | Raw output and canonical normalized surface are preserved separately. | `pipeline.py:44-76`; `artifacts.py:735-888`; verified `raw_model_output.npy` and `relative_surface.npy` | Neither array contains metres by itself. | "We preserve the model field before conversion and a separate normalized 0-to-1 surface for auditability." |
