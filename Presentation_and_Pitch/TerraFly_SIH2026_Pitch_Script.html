Introduction: Problem & Solution

🎙️ Presenter Spoken Script (30 Seconds)
"Respected Judges, in critical missions like disaster relief, military reconnaissance, or urgent infrastructure audits, responders often only possess a single 2D aerial snapshot or satellite photo. Today, estimating height or understanding the 3D terrain requires expensive multi-angle drone flights or LiDAR hardware taking hours to compute. We present TerraFly — an AI system that takes just one single 2D image and instantly reconstructs its 3D depth, estimates building and terrain heights in meters, and creates interactive cinematic flythrough videos in under 5 seconds."

🚨 The Critical Bottleneck

  • LiDAR Scanners: Prohibitively expensive ($10k-$100k) & heavy.
  • Photogrammetry (SfM): Demands 50+ overlapping images & high compute.
  • Zero 3D from Single Photos: Conventional GIS cannot extract 3D elevations from single legacy or reconnaissance photos.

💡 The TerraFly Breakthrough

  • Zero-Shot Monocular 3D: Generates dense metric 3D point clouds from 1 photo.
  • Metric Height Measurement: Live calibrated caliper measuring any building/cliff.
  • Cinematic Flythroughs: Generates 360° orbit, zoom, and terrain flyby videos.

Technical Approach & Architecture

🎙️ Presenter Spoken Script (45 Seconds)
"Our architecture combines state-of-the-art Vision Transformers with geometric ray casting. First, our Monocular Depth Engine extracts high-frequency structural depth boundaries. Second, our Metric Height Calibration module fits a ground plane equation to calculate precise relative heights in meters. Third, we unproject the 2D pixels into a 3D coordinate space and compute smooth parametric Bézier camera trajectories to render cinematic flythrough videos with occlusion handling — all exposed through an interactive real-time Three.js interface."

🧠 1. Monocular Depth ViT Backbone

Utilizes Vision Transformer backbones (Depth-Anything / MiDaS) with multi-scale feature aggregation to predict dense relative depth maps with sharp object boundaries.

📐 2. Metric Calibration & Ground Plane

RANSAC-based ground plane detection segments terrestrial base levels from vertical structures, converting relative disparity into real-world metric elevation (meters).

🎥 3. 3D Camera Trajectory Engine

Generates smooth 6-DOF orbital, spiral, and forward flythrough paths with depth-guided mesh inpainting to eliminate visual occlusion artifacts.

⚡ 4. Real-Time Web Stack

High-performance FastAPI backend with PyTorch acceleration paired with a responsive Three.js WebGL viewport for live point-cloud manipulation and export.

Feasibility, Challenges & Risk Mitigation

🎙️ Presenter Spoken Script (30 Seconds)
"The biggest challenge in single-view 3D is scale ambiguity and hidden occlusions when moving the camera. We overcame this by implementing ground-plane anchoring for metric scaling, and depth-aware mesh blending for smooth trajectory video rendering. TerraFly is engineered to run seamlessly on standard consumer hardware and edge devices without needing internet connectivity or cloud GPUs."
Technical Challenge Risk Factor TerraFly Mitigation Strategy
Scale Ambiguity Monocular images lack absolute physical distance markers. Reference height calibration & ground-plane regression algorithm.
Occlusion Holes Novel view flythroughs reveal unseen surfaces behind buildings. Depth-aware edge clamping & adaptive mesh point interpolation.
Hardware Constraints Heavy deep learning models cannot run in tactical field setups. FP16 half-precision, model quantization, and CPU-fallback pipeline.
